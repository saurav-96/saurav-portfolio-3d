I focus on small, understandable automations: take messy input, run it through clear steps, and hand back something a person can review. Below are three prototypes that run entirely in your browser — no data leaves the page and no API key is needed.

Research summariser

Problem
Long articles and notes take time to skim. I want the key sentences, recurring topics and any numbers pulled out for a quick first read.
Workflow
Paste textScore sentencesExtract topics & figuresHuman review
Technologies
Vanilla JavaScript, term-frequency scoring (extractive summarisation).
Limitations
It selects existing sentences — it does not understand or rewrite them, and works best on English prose. A real LLM step could be added server-side with a protected API key; that is not included here.

Structured content review

Problem
Drafts often go out with long sentences, vague wording or no clear call to action. A repeatable checklist catches the basics before a human edits.
Workflow
Paste draftRun rule checksScore & checklistEditor approves
Technologies
Vanilla JavaScript rules engine (readability, length, filler words, structure, CTA).
Limitations
Rules are heuristics, not a judge of quality, tone or facts. It flags things to look at; a person still decides.

Results will appear here.

Data clean-up pipeline

Problem
Copy-pasted lists (names, emails, product titles) are full of blanks, duplicates and inconsistent casing. Cleaning them by hand is slow and error-prone.
Workflow
Paste listChoose stepsRun in orderCopy result
Technologies
Vanilla JavaScript; composable transform steps; clipboard API.
Limitations
Operates on one item per line and on plain text only. Title-casing is simple and does not know names like "McDonald". Always spot-check the output.

How I work

A simple process, adapted to the kind of project.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We agree on the goal, audience, deadline and what “done” means.

    • Web: pages, content, devices
    • AI: the task being repeated
    • Design: brand, format, tone
  2. 02

    Plan

    I outline the solution and share it before building anything large.

    • Web: structure & wireframe
    • AI: workflow map & data
    • Design: moodboard & sketches
  3. 03

    Build & refine

    Work in small steps with check-ins, so feedback shapes the result early.

    • Web: responsive build
    • AI: prototype with review step
    • Design: drafts & revisions
  4. 04

    Test & deliver

    I test, fix, and hand over clean files with simple documentation.

    • Web: devices & accessibility
    • AI: edge cases & limits
    • Design: export-ready files
